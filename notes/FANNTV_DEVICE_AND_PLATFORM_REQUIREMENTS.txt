FANnTV product requirements

Support Android, iOS, Sony Android or Google TV, Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, and PC browsers.

Include four simultaneous devices per household. Offer one monthly or yearly add-on for two more slots. Admin sets prices in AZN globally or per household. On expiry, restore the four-device limit. Keep the base subscription term separate.

Allow the owner to set parental controls per device. Preserve stricter child profile limits. Keep household profiles, sessions, history, pricing and payments private. Apply a verified payment once to the household that owns the order.

Plan for over 100,000 accounts and about 10,000 simultaneous viewers. Test that when household A buys two slots, A can use six devices and B stays at four with unchanged billing and settings.

Implementation and store releases are pending.
